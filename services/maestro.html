iEHR Maestro: Orchestrating Healthcare AI Automation

Healthcare demands efficiency, accuracy, and security - but manual processes and outdated systems slow down innovation. iEHR Maestro empowers healthcare organizations by automating AI workflows, streamlining operations, and enhancing patient care with data-driven precision.

Artificial Intelligence (AI) offers unparalleled opportunities for businesses, but without automation, its full potential remains locked away. iEHR Maestro changes the game by combining AI-driven automation with flexibility, control, and scalability - perfect for technical teams who need powerful, adaptable workflow solutions.

Whether you prefer the precision of code or the speed of drag-and-drop, iEHR Maestro caters to your workflow preferences. Deploy on-prem for complete control or leverage cloud-based scalability - the choice is entirely yours.

Break Free from Limitations - Start Building Smarter AI Workflows​

Medical professionals and administrators struggle with fragmented systems, redundant tasks, and time-consuming documentation. iEHR Maestro transforms healthcare by integrating AI automation into clinical workflows, reducing administrative burden, and enhancing operational efficiency.

Rigid tools slow progress. iEHR Maestro is designed to streamline the creation and deployment of AI agents and MCP servers, with built-in support for major LLMs, vector databases, and an ever-expanding ecosystem of AI-powered solutions.

Automate Repetitive Healthcare Processes​

From patient scheduling and billing to medical record analysis and clinical documentation, AI-driven workflows eliminate manual tasks, reducing errors and accelerating response times.

Enhance Data-Driven Decision Making​

Integrate AI-powered analytics to detect trends, predict patient outcomes, and improve treatment plans through intelligent automation.

Ensure Compliance & Security​

Built-in FHIR support ensures seamless healthcare data interoperability, while HIPAA-compliant workflows maintain strict data privacy standards.

AI with Control, Not Just Hype​

AI should support medical expertise, not replace it. With iEHR Maestro, healthcare teams can combine AI intelligence with structured logic, ensuring accuracy, compliance, and control over sensitive patient data.

AI should empower, not overwhelm. With iEHR Maestro, businesses can blend AI intelligence with structured logic, ensuring predictability and control over outputs.

Leverage hundreds of integrations, custom code enhancements, and multi-agent AI workflows to boost operational efficiency. Achieve faster production cycles and flexible implementations without unnecessary complexity.

  • Integrate with enterprise healthcare apps
  • Customize AI-driven workflows for patient records
  • Seamlessly connect EHRs, imaging systems, and diagnostic tools

Drag. Drop. Deploy. No Limits.​

Don’t let complex administrative workflows slow down patient care. With visual state flows, reusable components, and rapid iteration cycles, healthcare providers can focus on treatment, not paperwork.

With iEHR Maestro, you can focus on AI-driven problem solving, not debugging repetitive scripts.

Integrate, Automate, Optimize - Built for Healthcare Professionals​

Medical facilities run on data, diagnostics, and precision - and iEHR Maestro ensures seamless interoperability with HL7, FHIR, and other healthcare standards.

  • Automate patient intake workflows
  • Enhance clinical decision support with AI models
  • Integrate electronic health records (EHRs) seamlessly

Code When You Need It, UI When You Don’t​

Unlike rigid automation platforms, iEHR Maestro delivers a hybrid approach - giving you the freedom to mix intuitive UI design with code-level precision.

  • Write in TypeScript or Python when customization demands extra power.
  • Built-in FHIR client for Python, ensuring healthcare data interoperability.
  • Extend workflows using npm or Python libraries for limitless expansion.
  • Paste cURL requests to directly integrate APIs.
  • Merge workflow branches dynamically - not just split them.

Run. Tweak. Repeat. Simplified AI Optimization.​

Eliminate long debugging cycles and optimize workflows effortlessly:

  • Re-run single steps without restarting the entire workflow.
  • Replay or mock data to avoid delays from external systems.
  • Debug faster with built-in logs aligned with your code.
  • Accelerate AI deployment using 1700+ ready-to-use workflow templates.

Connect Your Existing Tools - or Build Your Own​

Whether you rely on LLMs, databases, APIs, or vector stores, iEHR Maestro makes integration seamless. If the perfect component doesn’t exist yet, build your own custom module - without restrictions.
