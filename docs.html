Welcome to iEHR

The iEHR.ai platform is a AI-driven, FHIR®-native Unified Digital Health Platform (UDHP) that represents a transformative shift in healthcare technology. Unlike traditional, monolithic Electronic Health Records (EHRs), iEHR.ai embraces a modern, ecosystem-based approach that prioritizes interoperability, modularity, and innovation.

What is iEHR?​

iEHR is a comprehensive and advanced solution designed to revolutionize healthcare data management and integration, removing all pain points - including Infrastructure, interoperability, compliance, and security - involved in building, delivering, and supporting healthcare products and services. It offers robust functionalities spanning patient management, clinical workflows, and data analytics.

Key Characteristics & Goals of iEHR.ai​

  • Platform of Platforms
    Acts as a central hub that integrates diverse digital health tools and services.

  • Interoperability
    Enables seamless data exchange across systems using standards like FHIR®, enhancing connectivity for patients and clinicians.

  • Modular Architecture
    Offers scalable, adaptable infrastructure - far more flexible than rigid EHR systems.

  • Innovation Ecosystem
    Supports robust APIs and marketplaces to foster third-party app development and digital health innovation.

  • Improved Clinical Care
    Enhances care quality by unifying fragmented workflows and improving data accessibility.

  • Beyond Record Storage
    Provides infrastructure for a wide range of digital health functions - not just patient record management.

Advanced Features​

  1. FHIR® Datastore
    A FHIR®-compliant datastore designed to store and manage healthcare data, ensuring compatibility with other FHIR®-based systems and smooth data exchange.

  2. Automation Bots
    Automate workflows such as data synchronization, notifications, and clinical decision support. Bots enhance efficiency and reduce administrative burdens.

  3. Subscriptions
    Supports push-based subscriptions to FHIR® resources, enabling real-time updates and driving event-driven workflows.

  4. GraphQL API
    Offers flexible and efficient querying capabilities for managing healthcare data seamlessly.

  5. SDK & UI Components
    Features a developer-friendly SDK and library of reusable UI components for building custom applications with ease.

  6. Integrations
    Extensive integration support, including HL7, Stripe, OpenAI, and more, to connect with diverse healthcare systems and services effortlessly.

  7. SMART App Launch
    Enables seamless integration with third-party healthcare applications via the SMART App Launch framework.

  8. FHIRcast
    Facilitates real-time synchronization of data across multiple applications to enhance the user experience.

  9. Terminology Services
    Comprehensive services for managing and utilizing medical codes and vocabularies across various use cases.

  10. CLI Tools
    Command-line tools streamline the management and deployment of iEHR applications, empowering developers with efficient workflows.

Why Choose iEHR?​

  • Interoperability: Built on FHIR®, iEHR ensures seamless data exchange across systems and organizations.
  • Future-Ready: Designed to adapt to evolving healthcare standards and technologies.
  • Scalable: Provides robust solutions to meet the needs of small practices and large enterprises alike.
  • Customizable: Offers tools for tailoring the platform to unique operational requirements.

For additional details or assistance with iEHR, feel free to contact us.
