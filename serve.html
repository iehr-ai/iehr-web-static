Who We Serve

iEHR.ai empowers organizations across the healthcare ecosystem by delivering innovative, interoperable data solutions that drive efficiency, improve care quality, and enhance collaboration.

  • Entrepreneurs – We bridge the gap between concept and execution, helping organizations build scalable, interoperable solutions that turn ambitious visions into reality.
  • Researchers – Accelerate discoveries with standardized, structured, and queryable healthcare data that supports collaboration and insights.
  • Payers – Streamline data ingestion, ensure compliance, and optimize care management with secure and scalable FHIR®-native solutions.
  • Providers – Enhance patient care with unified data systems that create comprehensive, real-time ‘golden records’ for better clinical decision-making.
  • Health Exchanges – Build seamless interoperability networks that connect providers, payers, and researchers while maintaining security and compliance.
  • Health IT Organizations – Drive innovation with flexible, scalable, and secure data infrastructure that meets the evolving needs of modern healthcare.

By leveraging FHIR - the global standard for healthcare data exchange - our solutions empower organizations to transform healthcare delivery while ensuring data integrity, security, and accessibility.

Entrepreneurs​

Turn Concepts into Reality

The healthcare technology sector faces significant barriers to entry, including stringent regulatory requirements, interoperability challenges, high infrastructure costs, and data security concerns. Navigating compliance with healthcare-specific regulations like HIPAA or GDPR adds complexity for new entrants. Additionally, legacy systems within healthcare organizations often lack seamless data exchange capabilities, making integration difficult for modern solutions.

High development and implementation costs further deter smaller innovators, while concerns over cybersecurity and patient privacy necessitate robust protection measures. Addressing these challenges requires industry-wide collaboration, open standards like FHIR®, and scalable, secure technologies to streamline adoption and enhance healthcare innovation.

We are enabling founders and students to access iEHR.ai (Backend, SDK, UI, AI, Security, Connectivity, ...) free of charge for development and research, to bring their healthcare and AI ideas to life.

Researchers​

Accelerating Healthcare Discovery Through Interoperable Data

Healthcare research is often delayed by years due to data fragmentation, disconnected systems, and a lack of integration between clinical environments and research institutions. But with true interoperability, critical insights can be shared more efficiently - without compromising security or privacy.

With FHIR as the standard, iEHR.ai AI Data Platform transforms fragmented data into standardized, structured, and queryable datasets, ready for research and statistical analysis. Researchers can ingest data from multiple sources, synchronize data between clinical and research environments, and securely share insights with approved collaborators - all while maintaining compliance.

Payers​

Unify Data and Automate Systems for Enhanced Efficiency

Payers today are under immense pressure - data ingestion must be faster, regulatory landscapes are shifting, and competition is fiercer than ever. Forward-thinking payers are meeting these challenges head-on by adopting interoperability strategies rooted in the FHIR® open standards. This approach not only streamlines data ingestion but also ensures compliance, optimizes risk adjustment, and enhances care management and coordination.

Truly interoperable data and IT solutions should be secure, scalable, and modularly deployed when needed - maximizing system efficiency while driving improved care quality.

FHIR remains the gold standard for healthcare data exchange and interoperability. iEHR.ai AI Data Platform is the industry’s leading commercial FHIR-native solution for standardized, structured, and computable data. With the ability to ingest data from disparate legacy sources - including provider EHRs, claims, member systems, and third-party sources - iEHR.ai improves data quality and governance while ensuring secure, compliant access through consent and identification protocols.

Providers​

Harness Data for Empowered and Equitable Care

Managing vast volumes of clinical and personal data is an increasing challenge for healthcare providers in the era of data modernization. Creating unified IT systems free from vendor lock-in - systems that maintain up-to-date, comprehensive patient ‘golden records’ - is now achievable through FHIR®-native platforms.

Providers who embrace data modernization with FHIR experience cost-effective, real-time care delivery. Additionally, they are laying the groundwork for improved health equity by developing federated research data systems - systems that integrate into clinical practice sooner, when patients need it most.

iEHR.ai AI Data Platform, built on FHIR, ingests data from a wide range of legacy sources including EHRs, clinics, labs, pharmacies, research databases, and insurance payers. Through secure access protocols, compliance is upheld while enabling seamless data exchange.

Health Exchanges​

Building the Next Generation of Interoperable Systems

The need for unified, vendor-agnostic healthcare data systems is more pressing than ever. Health Exchanges are embracing FHIR®-native platforms to provide comprehensive, real-time access to patient records, enabling care providers to make informed decisions faster.

By modernizing with FHIR, Exchanges facilitate more efficient collaboration, interoperability, and research integrations - ultimately improving health outcomes.

iEHR.ai AI Data Platform acts as the connective tissue between disparate legacy sources, bringing clinics, labs, pharmacies, insurers, and providers into one secure and governed ecosystem.

Health It Organizations​

Fuel Growth and Innovation with FHIR®

Healthcare’s future is open, connected, and seamlessly integrated. iEHR.ai AI Data Platform ensures that Health IT organizations can deploy solutions at any level within their ecosystem - without disrupting existing workflows.

The era of data-driven healthcare demands flexibility, security, and scalability. By leveraging iEHR.ai FHIR-native infrastructure, IT organizations gain the ability to ingest, transform, and govern healthcare data from disparate systems while maintaining the highest standards of security and compliance.
