Distributed Systems Engineer

About the Role​

Embark on an exciting career journey with our fast-growing, early-stage healthcare tech company as we scale to support a backend serving millions of patients. As a key member of our expert team, you will take on the crucial challenge of developing and optimizing mission-critical healthcare systems. We are searching for a seasoned distributed systems engineer who excels in tackling rapid growth in data platforms and is passionate about delivering exceptional results.

This unique opportunity allows you to make a substantial impact by working alongside some of the world’s leading healthcare providers, taking ownership of transformative projects, and maintaining our commitment to excellence throughout our expansion. If you thrive in fast-paced, innovative environments and are ready to embrace this rewarding challenge, we want to hear from you.

What You Will Do​

  • Optimize Data Architecture: Design and refine data architecture to scale effectively for 100 million patients while ensuring top-tier performance, reliability, scalability, and availability.
  • Provide Technical Expertise: Deliver guidance on database technologies, sharding strategies, regional load balancing, and international data sovereignty to meet organizational goals.
  • Collaborate with Enterprise Partners: Work closely with key enterprise partners to align technical solutions with business requirements and priorities.
  • Influence Technical Leadership: Be a pivotal voice in shaping project strategies and decisions, contributing to our long-term growth and success.
  • Foster Team Culture: Help nurture an innovative, collaborative, and excellence-driven team culture as we continue to expand.

About You​

  • Education: Hold a degree in computer science, software engineering, or a related field.
  • Experience: Have 5+ years of expertise in developing and scaling data platforms and distributed systems.
  • Attitude: Possess a proactive, hands-on approach with a strong passion for scalability and high availability.
  • Location: Based in the Dublin or Galway area, ready for twice-weekly in-person collaboration.

Bonus Qualifications​

  • FHIR Knowledge: Familiarity with HL7 FHIR standards.
  • Tech Stack Expertise: Experience with AWS, Node.js, Postgres, and TypeScript.
  • Distributed Databases: Proficiency in at least one distributed database such as CockroachDB, Yugabyte, or Citus.
  • Startup/Big Tech Experience: Previous experience at an early-stage startup or in a large tech company.

About iEHR​

iEHR is an AI-driven, FHIR®-native Unified Digital Health Platform (UDHP), empowering healthcare organisations to drive innovation forward. At iEHR, we have a unique opportunity to impact the lives of patients and speed medical research and innovation.

Benefits​

  • Work From Home
  • Competitive compensation package

Join us in revolutionizing healthcare technology. If you are eager to help scale a platform serving millions of patients and be part of a team that values innovation and excellence, we’d love to hear from you. Reach out to us at career@iehr.ai.
